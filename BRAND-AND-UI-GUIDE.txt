eLINGKOD BAYAN — SHARED BRAND AND UI GUIDE
========================================
Identity: eLB | eLingkod Bayan – Los Baños | Report. Act. Resolve.
Academic/hackathon prototype; never imply official municipal endorsement.
Keep public/images/elb-mark.svg. Photography/illustrations are not proof of
municipal operations, government verification or measured community impact.

TYPOGRAPHY AND LAYOUT
Poppins, locally bundled in public/fonts; system sans fallback.
Body: 16px / 1.6; working controls 14px or larger; supporting labels 12px
minimum. Headings: 20–24px sections, 30–40px page titles; bold with clear
hierarchy. Sentence case for actions and labels; plain, specific language.
Line lengths generally 60–80 characters for explanation paragraphs.
Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48px. Cards use 16–24px padding,
16–24px corners; controls use 10–12px corners. Avoid arbitrary microtext.
Content max width 1344px; outer header/hero max width 1440px.
Desktop >=1024px; tablet 768–1023px; mobile <768px. Collapse navigation,
stack forms/cards, wrap labels/actions. Tables scroll within their own region,
never widen the page. Preserve long names, translated text and zoom usability.

COLOR SYSTEM — src/app/globals.css + src/styles/design-system.css
Use semantic utilities/variables, not new hardcoded page palettes.
Light: white card, white/pale-blue canvas, navy foreground/action (#153B63),
muted foreground #526B80, teal #08766C, gold #FFBF43.
Dark: canvas #101B29, card #172638, foreground #E9F2FA, muted #B6C9DA,
action #245F8D with white text, focus #85CEFF.
OLED: explicit #000000 canvas (including the protected shell), #0D0D0D cards,
#141414 nested/muted panels, #1B1B1B popovers/secondary surfaces,
foreground as Dark; visible #575757 borders and #737373 input boundaries.
All themes retain the eLB navy/teal/gold identity; never invert photos/logos.
Tokens: background/foreground; card/card-foreground; primary/primary-foreground;
secondary; muted/muted-foreground; accent; border/input/ring.
Status text uses success/destructive/warning-foreground. Filled actions use
success-solid/danger-solid rather than a bright dark-theme text color.
Status surfaces use success-soft/danger-soft/warning-soft. Always add labels
or icons; color alone must not convey status, selection or data meaning.
Fixed navy/image regions use on-brand-muted, on-brand-gold and on-brand-error; gold actions
use on-gold. These foregrounds do not follow the page's light/dark ink. Never
pair white text with a pale accent surface. Legacy white control backgrounds
must use card, and scoped legacy text variables must follow foreground tokens.
Sidebar initials and logout hover use the sidebar's dark card surface, with
white foreground; topbar avatars retain their page-surface pairing. Selected
filled tabs use primary-foreground for both labels and helper text. Gold focus
outlines belong on the fixed dark sidebar; content controls use ring. Text on
photos must pass against the rendered image plus overlay at every breakpoint;
use a local solid label backing where the photograph is too variable.

THEMES AND INTERACTION
Light, Dark and explicit OLED are available in the site-wide Appearance
control. Persist the allowlisted selection locally. Initialize it synchronously
in the document head before body paint; React must not be required for initial
colors. Unavailable storage falls back safely; do not make login depend on it.
Keep essential controls visible without hover. Hover is enhancement only.
Use >=44px primary input/button targets and visible keyboard focus (3px ring,
3px offset). Check default, hover, focus, selected, disabled, error and loading
states in every theme. Disabled controls keep readable labels and context.
Every action has an accurate label. Empty/error/denied states explain the next
available action. Do not grant protected access to fix an access-denied UI.
Dialogs have a title, keyboard focus, Escape/close action and return focus.
Respect reduced motion. Native selects are appropriate for simple choices.
Do not add a theme/chart dependency without a concrete unmet requirement.

CHARTS AND INSIGHTS
Use OverviewChart and the existing authorized aggregate results. Blue, teal,
purple and ochre distinguish series; unknowns are labelled explicitly.
Bars compare counts within a chart, never separate populations. Show exact
values, source, survey year/filter context and denominator/multiple-response
notes. Preserve supplied unknown percentages; never manufacture zero or 100%.
Provide keyboard/touch highlighting, visible selected state, status text,
and a complete table alternative. Empty and sparse data need honest messaging.
Keep the protected endpoint/role/barangay scope, consent and withdrawal rules.
Never expose identifiable census data or unscoped totals through chart props,
tooltips, labels, exports or hidden DOM. Public census analytics is deferred.

SIX CORE MODULES
1 Civic Reporting: citizen reports/tracking and authorized LGU case handling.
2 Online LGU Services: requests, documents, appointments and verified release.
3 Emergency Hotlines & Alerts: maintained directory/advisories; no fabricated
  official alerts, dispatch capability or unverified emergency contacts.
4 Municipal Projects & Services: current directory/preview; do not represent
  sample content as persisted project implementation.
5 Citizen Profiling: separate individual and establishment surveys, drafts,
  protected submissions/history and optional matching/program consent.
6 Worker Census & Social Profiling: protected records/imports and analytical
  source populations; workforce/social insights, opportunities and readiness.
  Suggestions do not guarantee eligibility/hiring or forward applications.
Authentication, permissions, notifications and administration support these
six modules. They are not additional core features. Demo records/listings and
simulated review events must stay visibly identified as demonstrations.

NAVIGATION APPEARANCE AND INSIGHT COMPOSITION
Appearance uses a 44px sun/moon/eclipse button in the public header and
protected topbar, always the rightmost utility. Group actions with 8px gaps;
the mobile navigation toggle sits immediately to its left. One activation
cycles Light -> Dark -> OLED -> Light; there is no menu, popover or switch.
The accessible name gives current theme and next action. Enter/Space/click/tap
cycle without moving focus; a polite live status announces the result.
Auth/unavailable shells retain a fixed top-right fallback without adding height.
Ignore hidden retained navigation when deciding whether that fallback is needed.
Notification utilities share the 44px target. Their labelled panel is viewport
clamped (16px gutter), dismisses on outside interaction/Escape and returns
keyboard focus on Escape. Portalling prevents filtered headers from trapping
fixed positioning; panel interactions must not count as outside clicks.

Protected dashboards lead with applied population/year/source and useful
recorded-value metrics, then complementary compact donut AND bar charts in a 2-column
grid (3 at >=1400px; 1 at <=650px). Filters follow overview; detailed tables,
methodology and exports follow filters. Each source gets its own totals and
units. No merged people/workforce/observations/assignments. Do not draw trends
without comparable time-series data or use pie charts for overlapping answers.
Cards:16px padding/radius; gap16px; Poppins16px chart headings,14px working
controls,12px supporting text; controls/targets remain >=44px even in compact
charts. Stable category-key colors use existing blue/teal/purple/ochre tokens;
unknowns use labelled ochre in Light and neutral pale gray in Dark/OLED.
Donuts require exhaustive mutually exclusive categories with one denominator;
include unknowns and never use multi-response skills/barriers as slices.
Use explicit segment boundaries and numbered callouts matching visible legends,
so repeated stable colors cannot merge categories. Show every category/count/
percentage; do not silently hide categories or fabricate an Other grouping.
Bars and exact values are visible by default. Compact tiles have no sort menus
or report disclosures; full accessible tables and methodology sit below filters.
Top5 categories are explicitly labelled; full values remain in accessible tables.
Employment status/type uses compact ranked top-five bars: its 15 supported
categories make a complete donut legend too tall for an overview. Keep smaller
exclusive distributions such as education as donuts alongside those bars.
Selection/hover/focus reports exact count, supplied percentage and denominator;
highlighting never pretends to apply a filter. Short findings use actual counts,
label ties and unknowns, and make no causal/population/hiring claims.

Select filters apply immediately; free text waits300ms. Keep applied context
with results, abort superseded requests and reject stale responses. Retain an
honestly labelled previous view while updating; exports use the applied response
and disappear during loading/errors. Reset clears incompatible filters. No fake
filter controls on server snapshots without an authorized filtering contract.
Reduce motion for users requesting it. Preserve photo/filled-surface contrast
rules and public census deferral.

OLED grouping uses visible borders/dividers and near-black surfaces, not shadows
alone. Preserve intentional 16px grid gaps and 20-28px section gutters; remove
accidental wrappers locally rather than compressing the whole site. Keep public
brand/photo surfaces recognizable. Fixed-primary content uses on-brand foregrounds
in every theme; page muted ink is unsuitable on a filled hero. Input outlines
use input (#71879B Light, #6E8AA3 Dark, #737373 OLED) for >=3:1 on their actual
surfaces; focus uses ring. Normal text requires >=4.5:1. Measure composited alpha,
gradients and actual photo pixels; inspect grouping in screenshots as well.
Inactive controls use full-opacity muted ink/surface with a dashed input boundary,
disabled semantics and not-allowed cursor. Do not fade their entire text layer.
Fixed-dark sidebar controls use the local card/ink pair. Include ancestor opacity
when measuring rendered foregrounds, even when the CSS color itself is opaque.

MATERIAL STATES AND KEYBOARD CONTINUITY
Shared keyboard focus is an unlayered 3px semantic ring with 3px offset so page
utility resets cannot erase it. The fixed navy sidebar owns a gold ring. Hidden
checkbox/radio choices put the ring on their visible label; never use the quiet
section-border token as a focus indicator. Disabled choice labels retain full
opacity, muted ink/fill and a dashed input boundary, including selection limits.
Every file chooser has a visible, keyboard-operable button named with its
document requirement; a hidden input alone is not a usable upload action.
Native dialogs are named by their visible heading. Survey Next/Previous/Skip
focuses and reveals the new step heading without changing answers; appearance
changes must not move focus or scroll. Emergency extension guidance uses the
semantic warning foreground on its actual card, with black OLED page canvas.
Printable request acknowledgements use white paper and dark readable ink in all
themes. Hide the appearance control in print; retain request facts and history.
Full-page public/account/service/opportunity containers use background; their
content cards use card. Do not paint the entire canvas with the card token:
Dark needs its stepped surfaces and OLED needs black around near-black cards.
Light destructive ink uses the deeper danger-solid value so it also passes on
the pale danger-soft surface; Dark/OLED retain their bright error foreground.
Uploaded-document actions wrap within narrow cards. Images may preview inline;
PDFs use an explicit protected Open/download action and the browser's viewer.
Do not leave a blank embedded PDF frame or relax sandbox/security boundaries to
make one work. Preserve original document bytes and server ownership checks.
