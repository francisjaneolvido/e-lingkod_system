INTERACTION
- Real actions should be labelled accurately, including when demo-only.
- Service preview pop-ups: keyboard closable via Escape and close button.
- Buttons/links must perform an action (no dead endpoints).
- Avoid mock statistics masquerading as live government figures.
- Never publish unverified emergency hotlines or fictional official advisories.
- Do not expose individually identifiable census information on public pages.

PRODUCT MODULES (APPROVED)
1 Civic Reporting: photo upload, permission-based GPS, tracking ID/status.
2 Online LGU Services: permits, taxes, clearances.
3 Emergency Hotlines & Alerts: PNP/BFP/Hospital/MDRRMO, push advisories.
4 Municipal Directory & Guide: requirements, fees, offices.
5 Job matching: jobseekers and businesses.
6 Worker and social-status census: privacy-conscious aggregated data.
