INTERACTION
- Real actions should be labelled accurately, including when demo-only.
- Service preview pop-ups: keyboard closable via Escape and close button.
- Buttons/links must perform an action (no dead endpoints).
- Avoid mock statistics masquerading as live government figures.
- Never publish unverified emergency hotlines or fictional official advisories.
- Do not expose individually identifiable census information on public pages.

PRODUCT MODULES (APPROVED)
1 Civic Reporting: saved photo uploads, permission-based GPS/manual location, tracking ID/status, citizen history, and protected LGU action desk.
2 Online LGU Services: permits, IDs, clearances.
3 Emergency Hotlines & Alerts: PNP/BFP/Hospital/MDRRMO, push advisories.
4 Municipal Projects & Services: offices, service directory, ongoing and future projects.
5 Job matching: SPES survey imports and hypothetical skills-to-opportunity suggestions; no application processing in this scope.
6 Worker and social-status census: occupation, employment status, socioeconomic survey data, and aggregated planning statistics.

Images in public/images/landing are AI-created illustrations for this prototype.
They are NOT photographs of actual LGU projects, events or service users.
